## Visible acceptance targets

The predecessor's D01–D13 and D17–D18 identify the selected defects. Their source
observations guide inspection; confirm actual local code before editing.

### Forest and distant ridge

Populate the still-bare far ridge around X=240..320 and improve surrounding
forest sectors while keeping the +X valley corridor open. V05 sector areas
corresponded to about 91–111 m² per tree before filtering; a 3 m minimum spacing
was not proof of dense forest. Use measured crown width, canopy overlap and
intentional glades to design coverage, not an arbitrary instance total.

Use fuller Picea abies crowns for nearer woodland edges and visible valley
flanks. Existing narrow Picea mariana can contribute to dense background stands;
do not display them as isolated dry needles on every foreground edge. Reuse
normalized v04 libraries and their credits, with credible height/width/rotation
variation and several irregular stand shapes. Do not reopen asset shopping or
mislabel the accepted assets as native-species certification.

Closed-forest patches should read as connected crown mass with irregular
skyline and occasional deliberate gaps. Transition through young trees and
undergrowth to meadow. Avoid repeated rows, a uniform ring, isolated trees spread
across otherwise bare smooth slopes, or a solid wall across the valley axis.
More instances alone do not resolve silhouette, depth or shading.

Preserve the regional valley/site layout. Limited **distant surface-form relief**
is allowed where needed to soften the simple Gaussian hill character, outside
the protected terrace and enclosure. Declare exact terrain groups/zones first;
use the resulting actual surface for every affected tree/grass root. Do not
remodel the garden, valley access or global terrain topology. Protect support
heights at house, paving, bed, orchard, gate and original foreground fence.

### Continuous meadow, not dark dots

Create a continuous low-to-mid vegetation layer with varied taller stems/seed
heads above it. Close the broad bare gaps between existing tall clumps. Visible
meadow should read as overlapping plant cover, including from low eye height
and orbit views, not identical circular tufts placed on a green floor.

V05 `_meadow()` used center-distance density bands, 2/0.5/0.12 levels and a
far scale of 0.5. Replace their visible bands/parcel-halo effect with world-space
irregular ecological patches and gradual forest/slope transitions. Preserve
meaningful clearings; eliminating artificial gaps does not mean covering every
path or deliberately open surface. Use more growth forms, thin broken clusters,
green/olive/dry layers and subtle local variation without repetitive circles.

Maintain the roughly 25–65 cm tall-meadow character, but judge ground/intermediate
cover independently of tallest tip height. Retain appropriate distant LOD cover
across all review sectors. C1-only culling that makes another orbit bare fails.
Actual density/LOD choices need sector renders, not a historical half-density rule.

### Natural short lawn and ground transition

V05 `_lawn_masters()` used five-point/three-triangle, nearly vertical strips with
small bends. Make believable bent/tapered blades with several lengthwise segments,
a restrained folded/midrib cross-section, twist, varied lean and some horizontal
growth. Avoid a field of identical upright ribbons. Keep the maintained 4–10 cm
scale and blend with lower thatch; do not inflate blades to make details visible.

Coordinate the PBR base, lower dry fibers and green blades: green/olive differences,
root-to-tip variation, roughness/translucency and real micro-shadows. Do not fake
brightness with emission or change scene lighting. Soften planting-edge transitions
where appropriate while retaining hard-surface clearances and usable gate/steps.

The original 2K Grass005 data may be in local work/downloads. Inspect actual bytes,
license and paths. Compare 1K/2K only with identical UV/scale/camera/density before
deciding whether a higher-resolution retained map helps. Save a chosen new map
under an honest V06 path/resolution; do not rename 1K as 2K or rewrite old assets.
Texture resolution is only one variable; it cannot replace blade shape/coverage.
New master variants/materials should remain reusable and compact. No uncontrolled
4K/8K downloads or new tree packs are needed.
