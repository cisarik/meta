## 8. Material conflicts (do not paper over)

These are why the first Worker is a Planner, not an implementation Worker.

1. **Inbound network.** Accepted truth: Tailscale-only remote access;
   Funnel disabled; no router port-forward; no public listener by default.
   A free public origin **supersedes** `SERVER.md` / ADR-0048 inbound
   sentences for a *second* origin. It must not Funnel the existing admin
   socket. `GET /health` and YouTube operator API must stay off the public
   origin.

2. **“Every caller” vs 401.** ADR-0049 says `GET /api/media` is
   published-only for every caller, but ADR-0048 remote callers without
   Serve identity never reach that route (`401`). Public internet needs a
   **new ingress mode** that allows identity-absent access to a
   **published-only policy subset**. Do not reuse `tailscale_uds` with
   optional identity.

3. **No personal libraries.** ADR-0053 forbids them. “Each user has a
   gallery” must be specified as contributor-scoped unpublished *read* plus
   a workspace surface (ADR-0054 rhyme), or Michal must explicitly accept
   a new ownership model. `logical_media` has no owner column.
   Attribution exists on upload sessions, X claims, YouTube claims, and
   alias rows.

4. **Ordinary Gallery vs work gallery.** Today ordinary mapped users do
   **not** see their unpublished uploads in Gallery. Upload completion
   tells them it awaits review. Michal’s “add to their gallery” is a
   **gap**. Planner must name the surface (workspace gallery / My
   submissions / Manage-media-for-self) without widening `GET /api/media`
   to unpublished for ordinary or public callers.

5. **Admin alias read.** ADR-0062 caller-private vs Michal’s “admin sees
   others’ aliases.” New capability, audit, and UI. Do not leak aliases
   to other ordinary users or to public.

6. **Propose analysis.** Ordinary users lack `analysis.run`. Auto-analysis
   flag stays default off and must not be the implementation of “propose.”
   Proposal should be durable, fail-closed, administrator-visible, and
   must not enqueue NIM by itself.

7. **Attach.** Website Gallery Attach is companion-hosted today
   (ADR-0063). Public “attach” means at least: (a) connect a later
   companion to the **public** origin and attach published memes into X;
   (b) view/search on the public website. Planner must not assume public
   people get Tailscale companion review chrome.

8. **Movies.** Companion excludes movie workflows. Public Publish of
   movies is website/admin, then public view. Do not reopen frozen movie
   identification (ROADMAP) inside this whole.

9. **Loopback-first process** remains. Public ingress, if accepted, is a
   distinct listener and a distinct policy table. Hiding buttons is not
   authorization.

10. **Premium gallery invariant.** Public view is still FrameNest Gallery,
    not a throwaway marketing page. Do not freeze accepted Gallery/Details
    visual MVP unless a concrete defect is found.
