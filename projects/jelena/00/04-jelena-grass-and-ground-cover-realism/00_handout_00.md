## 7. Grass design brief

Improve grass on the existing eligible ground of the main exterior scene. “Whole scene” does not authorize changes to scene B, house interiors, trees, existing accepted layout or every plant species.

Required qualities:

1. Tapered, gently bent blades with variation in curvature, orientation and silhouette. Avoid upright rectangular strips, uniformly pointed spikes and identical billboard stars.
2. Convincing clumps with varied blade lengths and widths; coherent scale in metres relative to the steps and pavers. Vary within restrained ranges, not random extremes.
3. Clearly denser ground cover, with layered blades and some readable soil between irregular patches. Avoid a uniform carpet, military rows, repeated clump grids or dense bushes masquerading as grass.
4. Natural restrained green variation, darker roots and plausible sunlit tips. Leaf lighting/transmission should preserve form, not produce neon or plastic highlights.
5. Distribution informed by terrain and existing grass eligibility. Preserve non-grassy surfaces and intentional clearances. Do not fill accepted access/east clearances simply because a broad terrain mesh extends beneath them.
