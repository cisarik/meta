## 3. Accepted direction (Cooperator-selected, 2026-08-27)

Michal selected candidate shape A ("Followable Spine" synthesis). The plan
must cover all six elements; none may be silently dropped:

1. **Per-role minimum-reading spine, owned in AP.md.** For each role
   (COOPERATOR, ORCHESTRATOR, WORKER), the short normative list of AP.md
   sections/RF families that must be read before first exchange in a whole,
   plus which projections are required versus reference-on-demand. The spine
   lives inside AP.md (semantic ownership, ADR-0013); INTUITION.md and the
   handbooks may point to it but never own it.
2. **Three-class detectability classification.** Every normative sentence
   classed as: (1) artifact-detectable — its violation leaves evidence in a
   report, record, or repository artifact; (2) behavioral-normative — binding
   but visible only in behavior; (3) undetectable-and-unenforced. Class 3 is
   demoted to advisory or deleted, with a per-item disposition. Class 2 is
   kept in its single owner and never restated elsewhere. The plan defines
   the classification method, the coverage route over AP.md §§1–19 + RF
   capsules + all projections, and the disposition format.
3. **Restatement → pointer conversion (one-rule-one-home).** Paraphrases of an
   owned rule in projections/handbooks/FAQ become a pointer plus at most one
   orientation sentence. Known instance to inventory and convert: the
   "one initial planning cycle + at most one targeted revision" rule (~8
   surfaces: AP.md twice, PROMPT_CONTRACTS.md twice, both handbooks, FAQ,
   ADR-0011, ADR-0013). Conversion preserves normative force exactly; it is
   re-homing, never weakening.
4. **`00_notes.md` convention.** A per-logical-whole Orchestrator notes file
   (fixed name beside the handout; append-only, dated, Orchestrator-only,
   evidence-never-authority) formalized as an AP-run convention. The plan
   decides and justifies its home(s): AP_ORCHESTRATOR.md operational
   projection, ARTIFACT_LIFECYCLE.md row, and/or an optional companion under
   RF-19 — and states explicitly that the filename is local grammar, never a
   universal required AP field.
5. **Detection-surface rule for future rules.** A governance sentence in
   AP.md: a newly added normative rule must name the artifact where its
   violation becomes visible, or be classified advisory (the emoji lesson,
   formalized).
6. **Adopted-and-testable criteria.** The plan defines what "adopted and
   testable" means for the later separate FrameNest pin-adoption whole, and
   drafts the numbered plain-language field-test script Michal will run
   (fresh-Orchestrator spine-only reading; named decision and notes location;
   coordinate echo in one Worker report; "what must I read" answerable with
   one short list).
