## 5. Locked Cooperator decisions — do not reopen

* AP keeps exactly three persistent roles: COOPERATOR, ORCHESTRATOR, WORKER.

  session profiles (implementation-planning, Fresh Implementation Worker,
  Fresh Independent Audit, and similar).

  Orchestrator conversation history. A subagent spawned inside the parent

  implementation rationale beyond the candidate and control matrix, is not
  independent audit.
* Internal delegation is never the default, remains visible through
  Orchestrator routing and Cooperator-legible grants/verdicts, and must not be
  represented as independent audit.

### Lock 2 — Emoji and Slovak are not universal AP fields

* ADR-0017 rejected “Slovak, emoji, or a particular trace implementation as
  required AP fields.”
* Any signaling vocabulary is opt-in advisory / project-owned presentation.
* Do not add emoji, language, or Meta-grammar fields to
  `PROMPT_CONTRACTS.md`.
* Do not make a signaling table a Worker-authority gate.

### Lock 3 — `INTUITION.md` is a brief projection; meaning lives in `AP.md`

* `AP.md` remains the sole live normative protocol and semantic owner
  (ADR-0013).
* `INTUITION.md` is a new subordinate artifact in the AP source root (the

  Orchestrator to design it intuitively; the planner specifies the exact

  bound: **at most 200 lines**, principles and dispatch rules, canonical

  prompt-contract schema.
